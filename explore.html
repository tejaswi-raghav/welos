Wind generation

Vertical-axis wind turbine

Captures wind from multiple directions while maintaining a compact vertical footprint.

A helical rotor drives a direct-drive permanent-magnet generator. A fail-safe brake stops it for faults, extreme wind and service, independent of any cloud connection.

03
Protective chassis

Enclosure and structure

IP-rated enclosures protect the control and power electronics while a hinged door keeps everything reachable for maintenance.

Devices mount on a grounded backplate with segregated cabling. Four posts carry the canopy loads down into the base.

05
Rain catchment

Gutter and first flush

Rain runs off the panel canopy into a perimeter gutter. A first-flush diverter rejects the initial runoff, which carries the most dust and debris.

Rain sensing triggers the diversion before anything reaches storage.

Capture rate
50 L/min

Design target from the product roadmap.

06
Water storage

Protected storage tank

Harvested rainwater is held in an opaque, protected tank with screened vent, overflow and drain connections.

Sensors track level, flow, pressure, turbidity, conductivity and leaks so the controller can automate safely.

Capacity
300 L

Design target from the product roadmap.

Multi-stage treatment

Filter and UV train

Water moves through sequential treatment stages before it is used.

  • Carbon filtration
  • UV-C disinfection

Supply

08
Pump & delivery

Variable-speed pump

Pressurises treated water on demand for irrigation, panel cleaning and other approved non-potable loads.

One outlet returns to the canopy: a low-flow manifold that rinses the panels when the controller detects soiling.

09
Structural foundation

Base frame

Supports the integrated system and provides a stable platform for its mechanical components.

A base plate and frame sit on four mounting feet. Canopy posts and the turbine mast land directly on it.

How energy moves

HARVEST.

welos captures available renewable energy from its environment.

Generation serves household loads first. Surplus charges the battery, then powers water treatment or exports to the grid. The battery holds a 20% reserve floor.

How water moves

TREAT.

Water moves through an integrated treatment architecture before use.

  • Rain on canopy
  • Gutter + first flush
  • Storage
  • Filter + UV train
  • Pump
  • Output
The connection

NOT SEPARATE TECHNOLOGIES.

ONE INTEGRATED SYSTEM.

Renewable power runs the pump and treatment. Stored rainwater rinses the panels when the controller detects a soiling loss above 15%.
